# Kyntral Contributor License Agreement — DRAFT

**CONFIDENTIALITY STATUS:** Public draft for review.  
**LEGAL STATUS:** Not operative. Not legal advice. Requires qualified counsel review before adoption.

Kyntral intends to use a contributor agreement that preserves contributor authorship while giving the canonical project enough rights to distribute the contribution under Kyntral's mixed licensing model, operate commercial products, and make future licensing changes where appropriate.

The final agreement is expected to cover:

1. contributor representation that they have the right to submit the contribution;
2. a broad copyright license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, sublicense, and commercialize the contribution;
3. an express patent license for patent claims necessarily infringed by the contribution;
4. permission to distribute the contribution under current or future Kyntral licenses;
5. preservation of contributor authorship/attribution where reasonably practicable;
6. no transfer of the Kyntral trademarks or canonical governance authority;
7. no obligation for Kyntral to accept or use a contribution;
8. disclosures for employer-owned or third-party material;
9. a process for individual and corporate contributors.

Until counsel approves a final CLA and the acceptance workflow records assent, **external code contributions MUST NOT be merged**. Issues, design proposals, documentation suggestions, test reports, and review feedback remain welcome.
