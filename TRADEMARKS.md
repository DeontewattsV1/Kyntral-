# Kyntral trademark policy — draft

Software licenses in this repository do not grant trademark rights.

Pending formal trademark review, community projects may factually describe
compatibility using phrases such as:

- "compatible with the Kyntral Protocol"
- "built with Kyntral"
- "Kyntral-compatible"

Do not use the Kyntral name or visual identity in a way that implies an
unofficial product is an official Kyntral release, certified extension, hosted
service, or endorsed partner.

Names such as "Kyntral Cloud", "Kyntral Pro", "Kyntral Enterprise", and use of
the official Kyntral mark for third-party products require written permission.

This draft is a project policy, not a representation that a trademark
registration has been obtained.
