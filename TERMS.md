## Intended service boundary

Kyntral mediates narrowly scoped requests between an authenticated AI client and a paired user device. Installing a Shortcut, plugin, SDK, or extension does not itself grant device authority.

Users remain responsible for authorizing only devices and workflows they control, complying with third-party service terms and applicable law, protecting OAuth tokens and device access, and reviewing destructive or sensitive operations before authorization.

## Privacy

The intended control plane does not require private workflow payloads merely to authorize a device action. See `PRIVACY.md`.
